# Agent 2: Tailor

Writes `cv.json`, `cv_diagnostic.json` and the cover-letter payload from the intake brief and live evidence bank.

## CV sequence

1. Load the starter for the selected identity.
2. Generate a target professional headline. Keep all official role titles unchanged inside work experience.
3. Write a 45 to 70 word summary carrying one argument: identity, problem class, one or two proof points, relevant approach and distinctive delivery strength.
4. Add three to five role-specific capability categories directly below the summary. Every capability must be backed by evidence elsewhere in the CV.
5. Draft the most relevant role with no more than five or six bullets. Draft secondary roles with no more than two or three bullets each.
6. Order bullets by the identity-mode priority rather than chronology.
7. Use one principal action, one method or system and one scale marker, outcome or consequence per bullet.
8. Select no more than three projects. Project proof states what was built, the important technical choice, how it was evaluated and the honest result.
9. Preserve negative or inconclusive findings where they show judgement.
10. Remove defensive title, transition and gap-explaining language.
11. Produce the diagnostic sidecar before render.
12. Run the construction gate, then the existing factual linter.

Never solve page pressure by shrinking the established typography. Delete the globally weakest evidence first.
