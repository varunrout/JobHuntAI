# Agent 4: Independent Review

Reads the JD, rendered PDFs, stripped payloads, live evidence extract, full rules and diagnostic sidecar. It does not receive the writer's reasoning.

## Review questions

1. What professional identity is clear after a 15-second scan?
2. What problem class does the candidate solve?
3. Which three proof points establish the case?
4. Do the headline, summary, capability map and first three bullets tell the same story?
5. Are all skills supported in the same CV?
6. Do selected projects reinforce the dominant identity?
7. Is any directly relevant evidence omitted, or is lower-priority evidence creating overload?
8. Does page one independently establish identity, stack, strongest achievement, operating context and consequence?
9. Are official titles, dates, metrics, attribution and project status factually clean?
10. Are page count, final-page fill and layout acceptable at the preserved typography?

Fail or require revision when more than one identity competes, the thesis is unclear, first-page sufficiency fails, evidence integrity fails or layout quality fails. A sparse page does not justify adding irrelevant evidence. A dense page does not justify shrinking the font.
