# JobHuntAI Networking Rules

These rules are binding for all JobHuntAI networking, connection requests, first-touch outreach, follow-ups and networking-agent output.

If an older networking instruction conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## 1. Core personalisation test

A message only earns a reply if the reader can tell in the first five words that it was written for them specifically, not copied with a name swapped in.

Before releasing any first-touch message, run this test:

> If the person's name and title were removed, would the opening sentence still make sense for almost anyone at that company?

If yes, rewrite it.

The opening must anchor to something real and checkable about that specific person, such as their exact team, remit, current role, published work, previous interaction with Varun, or direct relationship to the target vacancy.

Do not use generic company praise as personalisation.

## 2. Banned networking language

Do not use:

- `I hope this finds you well`
- `I'd love to pick your brain`
- `I'm passionate about`
- `I've been following your career`
- `came across your profile`
- `caught my attention`
- `great to connect`
- `would love to connect`
- generic compliments aimed at the company rather than the person
- generic networking-advice phrasing that could be reused for another employee unchanged

Natural conversational language is preferred over polished corporate language.

## 3. Mandatory first-touch structure

Every first-touch networking message uses exactly three moves, nothing else:

1. **Anchor**: the real, checkable reason Varun is reaching out to this specific person. Usually this is the role, their exact team, a previous interaction, or a specific piece of their work.
2. **Evidence**: ONE relevant true fact about Varun's work, in one clause, selected for relevance to that person's role. It does not need to be Varun's strongest achievement overall.
3. **Soft close**: an invitation to connect or one specific low-effort question. No larger ask on message one. Include an easy exit such as `No need to reply if the timing is off` or a natural equivalent.

Do not add a fourth move, biography paragraph, CV summary, referral request, or multiple questions.

## 4. Evidence integrity

Message evidence must come from `MASTER_PROFILE.md`, verified project evidence, the saved JD, or a user-confirmed fact from the current workflow.

Never invent, infer, round up or embellish:

- metrics
- results
- ownership
- deployment status
- client work
- football-club experience
- relationship strength
- internal knowledge
- referrals

Use only the evidence explicitly supported by the source of truth.

## 5. Required inputs before drafting

Before drafting a new networking message, establish all three of the following:

1. the contact's exact current title and team;
2. the role and company being used as the outreach anchor;
3. the one true evidence point to use.

Do not proceed on assumptions. Verify what can be verified from available sources. If a required fact genuinely cannot be resolved, ask Varun rather than inventing it.

## 6. Contact-type weighting

### Recruiters and Talent Acquisition

Lead with role fit and relevance.

Do not put sponsorship or visa questions in the opener. Ask work-authorisation questions only after the recruiter has engaged, because sponsorship is a viability issue but not a first-touch hook.

### Peers and adjacent team members

Anchor to their actual work or team and ask about the real day-to-day, team interface, technical environment or transition into the field.

Never ask a peer for a referral on message one.

Never ask a peer about sponsorship or immigration policy.

### Hiring managers and functional decision-makers

The message may carry both the fit case and a concise sponsorship/work-authorisation question when that person genuinely owns or influences the hiring decision.

Keep the question low-effort and specific.

### Senior leaders

Use the shortest and sharpest version.

Anchor to something uniquely relevant to their remit. Do not give a long background summary or ask for a referral in the first message.

### Warm contacts

When Varun has already spoken with or corresponded with the person, lead with the shared history or explicit prior invitation to keep in touch.

Do not write to a warm contact as though they are a cold lead.

## 7. LinkedIn connection-note constraints

LinkedIn connection notes must:

- stay under 300 characters;
- use British English;
- contain no em dash;
- follow the same anchor, one-evidence-point, soft-close logic;
- avoid generic greetings and filler where character count is tight.

Character count must be checked before release.

## 8. Purpose and outreach volume

Networking is mandatory for P0 and P1 submitted applications unless a documented reason makes outreach inappropriate.

The purpose is relevance and visibility, not volume.

Do not contact more than three people at one company for one vacancy before receiving a response.

Do not repeat the same message across multiple employees. Each person's anchor and question must be specific to them.

## 9. Referral and relationship rules

Referral asks are allowed only after meaningful interaction or when the person explicitly offers.

A LinkedIn profile view is not a reply or hiring signal.

A connection acceptance is logged as acceptance, not advocacy.

Do not exaggerate a previous interaction into a relationship stronger than the evidence supports.

## 10. Follow-up rule

One follow-up after 7 to 10 days is the default maximum without engagement.

A follow-up should continue the existing person-specific thread. It must not reset into a generic networking template.

Stop after one follow-up unless the contact re-engages.

## 11. Logging and closure

Every networking action must be logged under the JobHuntAI Networking system before it is considered complete.

Retain exact or faithful message text, contact identity, contact type, role anchor, evidence used, outcome, next action and date according to `NETWORKING_LOGGING_STANDARD.md`.

Close the active networking plan when the application is rejected, withdrawn, filled or inactive, while retaining relationship history for future relevant opportunities.

## 12. Release checklist

Before any networking message is released, confirm all of the following:

- [ ] The first five words are person-specific.
- [ ] The anchor is real and checkable.
- [ ] Exactly one evidence point is used.
- [ ] The evidence is true and source-backed.
- [ ] The close is low-effort.
- [ ] The first-touch message contains no referral ask unless explicitly offered first.
- [ ] Sponsorship handling matches the contact type.
- [ ] The message contains none of the banned generic phrases.
- [ ] A LinkedIn connection note is under 300 characters.
- [ ] British English is used.
- [ ] No em dash is used.
- [ ] Removing the name/title would not leave a message reusable for another employee unchanged.
