# JobHuntAI Networking Rules

1. Networking is mandatory for P0 and P1 submitted applications unless a documented reason makes outreach inappropriate.
2. The purpose is relevance and visibility, not volume.
3. No invented relationship, internal knowledge or referral.
4. Message evidence must come from MASTER_PROFILE and the saved JD.
5. Never lead with sponsorship in a connection note.
6. Ask work-authorisation questions early after recruiter acceptance because it is a fatal viability issue.
7. Technical peers are asked about the work and team, not HR policy.
8. Do not contact more than three people at one company for one vacancy before receiving a response.
9. Do not repeat the same message across multiple employees.
10. One follow-up after 7–10 days is the default maximum without engagement.
11. Every action must be logged in Networking before it is considered complete.
12. A LinkedIn profile view is not treated as a reply or hiring signal.
13. A connection acceptance is logged as acceptance, not advocacy.
14. Referral asks are allowed only after meaningful interaction or when the person explicitly offers.
15. Close the plan when the application is rejected, withdrawn, filled or inactive, but retain the relationship history.
